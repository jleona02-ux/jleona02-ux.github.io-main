The Red Mamak Plastic Stool, is actually very self-explanatory, it is simply an easily reproducible and relatively inexpensive red chair that is used in almost every establishment that I can find back home. Im not entirely sure on what brand do they use in particular, but almost all of these chairs are identical towards each other, having a glossy plastic finish, and a vivid red colour that can be spotted from a mile away.

This particular type of red chair, has always been scattered throughout both my childhood home and the homes of many family members. An ubiquitous tool for gathering, this chair has been used for countless family gatherings, often using these chairs for additional guests that are overflowing the living room. I fondly recall spending many late nights sitting on these chairs, often switching the position and posture of my chair out of sheer boredom. I also remember trying to kill time with anyone that is around my age, trying to talk about completely miscellanous topics, trying to avoid the dread that is talking to an particular auntie or uncle that I wasn’t completely fond of. I also used to play so much mobile games with my distant relatives on these chairs, often crossing my legs, putting my phone beneath the table, and having the time of my life there.

More importantly, I would also remember these family gatherings ending with us trying to stack as much of these chairs as possible into the corner of the living room, or even to an relatively dusty closet, with the smell of cooked food and cigarettes permeating the space, a sign that the night is almost over.
