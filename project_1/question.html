so, what's up with this?

When I was combing through a myriad of are.na based websites, I stumbled upon the realization that, everything is about curation? Yes, the topics are very particular, we can talk about traffic signs, a random Japanese man’s toy collection, or even potholes, but they all serve a singular purpose, which is to document and preserve the history that’s often tied with these objects. But why do we want to preserve these objects so much? Why do we put such an emphasis towards the act of preserving, even spending the majority of our free-time doing so? Aren't our names, and even our beloved objects, will be forgotten within a generation or two?

That’s why this *small* website exists to explore the emphasis of preservation towards such mundane objects. I am trying to put an emphasis on it through the exploration of how the random items that are in both my childhood home and college room hold so much meaning to my heart.
